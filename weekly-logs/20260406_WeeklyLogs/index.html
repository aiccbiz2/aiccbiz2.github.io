  • 헤드라인: clamp(42px, 7vw, 72px), font-weight 700
  • 섹션 padding 100px 24px / max-width 1100px
  • 버튼: pill형 (border-radius 980px)
  • 스크롤 fade-in: opacity 0→1 + translateY
